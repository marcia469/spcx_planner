Excess = daily volume minus normal volume, summed from the unlock day. Each unlock's window runs until the day before the next one, so no day is counted twice; unlocks two or three sessions apart (Oct 22 and Oct 26) count as one release. Below-normal days subtract. Normal volume defaults to each unlock's clean 10-day median before it (no index, earnings or unlock days). Excess volume is a proxy: it shows how much extra trading took place, not who sold, so holders selling slowly inside normal volume would not show up here.

Index funds trade at the close, so daily volume (which includes the auction) is the right measure; excess on the day typically runs above modelled demand because other traders take the other side and pre-position. Analyst demand uses the planner's per-index OS% inputs; JPM demand is from its Oct 22 note. CRSP transitions are spread over several days, so its single-day ratio understates the trade.
